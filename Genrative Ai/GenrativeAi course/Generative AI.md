## ChatGPT: Capabilities & Limitations (Updated)

### What ChatGPT Does Well

- **Content Creation** – Generates clear slide text, outlines, and speaker notes
    
- **Structured Design Thinking** – Proposes logical layouts, slide flow, and storytelling
    
- **Brand Alignment** – Follows provided brand, tone, and formatting guidelines
    
- **Data Preparation** – Creates tables, chart data, formulas, and analysis
    
- **Image Generation** – Produces custom visuals and illustrations on demand
    

### Current Limitations

- **No Native Slide Interaction** – Cannot apply animations or transitions directly inside PowerPoint
    
- **Tool-Dependent Export** – Direct `.pptx` creation depends on integrations or plugins
    
- **Manual Media Insertion** – Images, videos, and embeds often require manual placement
    
- **No Live Data Feeds** – Charts do not auto-refresh with real-time data
    
- **Execution vs Design** – Designs _what_ to build; users finalize _how_ it’s implemented
