
# TF-IDF

### The Selection

**TF-IDF** = Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency

> A smarter way to represent the importance of words in documents.

---

## The Problem with Simple Counting

A standard **Document-Term Matrix (DTM)** counts how often words appear.

But common words create a problem:

- **"the"**
- **"is"**
- **"and"**

These words appear everywhere.

❌ They occur frequently  
❌ But they tell us very little about the topic

---

## TF-IDF Solves This

TF-IDF looks at **two things**:

### 1. TF — Term Frequency

How often does a word appear in **this document**?

**Higher TF → More important**

### 2. IDF — Inverse Document Frequency

How unique is the word across **all documents**?

**Higher IDF → More unique**

---

## The Magic of TF-IDF ✨

TF-IDF gives a **high score** to words that are:

> **Frequent in one document**
>
> **but rare in the rest**

This helps identify the words that best describe a document.

---

## Example: 100 Animal Articles 🐋

Imagine we have **100 articles about animals**.

### Word: `"the"`

Appears in:

**100 / 100 articles**

➡️ Not useful for distinguishing documents  
➡️ **Low IDF**  
➡️ **Low TF-IDF**

---

## Example: `"Whale"` 🐋

Suppose **"Whale"** appears many times in Article #5.

But it appears very rarely in the other **99 articles**.

➡️ High TF  
➡️ High IDF  
➡️ **High TF-IDF**

### Therefore:

**"Whale" becomes a key topic of Article #5.**

---

## Why Does This Matter?

TF-IDF helps computers understand **what a document is about**.

For example:

| Document | Important Words |
|---|---|
| Algorithms | Bellman-Ford, graph, shortest path |
| Food | Pizza, cheese, restaurant |

Even though both documents may contain:

> **"the"** hundreds of times

TF-IDF gives more importance to the **distinctive words**.

---

## Where Is TF-IDF Used?

### 🔎 Search Engines
Find documents relevant to a query.

### 🎯 Recommendation Systems
Identify important topics and similarities.

### 📄 Text Classification
Represent documents as numerical features.

### 🔍 Information Retrieval
Rank documents based on word importance.

---

# Key Idea

## TF-IDF = Importance of a Word

**High TF-IDF**

→ Frequent in this document  
→ Rare across other documents  
→ **Strong indicator of the document's topic**

---

# Remember

> **TF tells us:**  
> *"How often does this word appear here?"*

> **IDF tells us:**  
> *"How rare is this word everywhere else?"*

### TF × IDF = TF-IDF
