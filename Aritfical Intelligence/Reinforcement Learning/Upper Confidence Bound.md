The **Upper Confidence Bound (UCB)** algorithm is ==a deterministic reinforcement learning method used to solve the exploration-exploitation dilemma==. 
It operates under the core principle of **"optimism in the face of uncertainty,"**
which means it prioritizes trying actions that have highly uncertain rewards.

## Note:

**Upper Confidence Bound (UCB) explores actions/paths with uncertain rewards**, not actions whose rewards are already certain.
