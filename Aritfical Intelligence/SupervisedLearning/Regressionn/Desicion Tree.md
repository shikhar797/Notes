A **decision tree** is ==a supervised machine learning algorithm used for both **classification** (predicting categories) and **regression** (predicting numerical values)==. 

It works like an upside-down flowchart that splits data into smaller subsets based on specific features or conditions, 
ultimately leading to a final decision or prediction.


- **Root Node:** The very top node representing the entire dataset, which undergoes the initial split.

- **Internal / Decision Nodes:** Nodes where the data splits further based on specific questions or conditions.

- **Branches:** Paths connecting nodes, representing the outcome of a decision or condition.

- **Leaf Nodes:** Terminal nodes at the bottom that give the final classification or predicted value; they do not split any further.
