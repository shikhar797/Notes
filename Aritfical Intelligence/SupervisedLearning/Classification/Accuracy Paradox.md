
The **accuracy paradox** ==occurs when a predictive model has a high accuracy score, but it is actually useless==. 

This happens when data sets are unbalanced, meaning one group appears much more often than the other. 
The model simply guesses the main group every time to look good.

For eg ->
in our dataset 95% item is true 
and our model simpily written true every time then it is 95% accurate but in reality it is of no use
