5.Advertising

Machine Learning (ML) in advertising ==uses algorithms to analyze vast data, enabling hyper-personalized experiences, precise audience targeting, predictive analytics for customer behavior, and real-time campaign optimization==, ultimately boosting engagement and ROI by delivering the right ad to the right person at the right time. Key applications include dynamic content, automated bidding, fraud detection, and personalized recommendations, making ads more relevant and efficient.
